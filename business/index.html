The Business Opportunity

Business Model

The USANA business model centers on direct sales and relationship building. Instead of spending revenue on traditional retail overhead and advertising, compensation is paid directly to independent distributors who generate product sales and develop organizations of other distributors and preferred customers.

Ethical Business Practice

Compliance is mandatory. As a distributor, you must:

  • Never make claims that products can cure, treat, diagnose, or prevent any disease.
  • Never guarantee income or present hypothetical earnings as typical.
  • Always represent the time and effort required to build a sustainable business authentically.
